# Commercial Licensing for Flama G2P

While the community/source-available edition of Flama G2P is free for personal, educational, and research use under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0, any commercial application requires a separate commercial license from Flama.

## When do you need a Commercial License?

A commercial license is mandatory if Flama G2P is used within, integrated with, or serves any of the following:

- Paid desktop or mobile software
- Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platforms
- Subscription-based products or services
- Paid APIs or web services
- Commercial websites
- Enterprise, corporate, or internal business software
- Software or tools supplied to customers or clients
- Products or services that directly or indirectly generate revenue
- Commercial products that embed, depend on, or bundle Flama G2P

## Inquiries & Contact

Commercial licensing terms and agreements are established on a case-by-case basis. 

For commercial licensing inquiries, contact the Flama project owner.
